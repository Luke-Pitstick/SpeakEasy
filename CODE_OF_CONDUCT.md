# Code of conduct

SpeakEasy adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link is the project's code of conduct. This page summarizes it and explains
how to report a problem.

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in SpeakEasy should be free from harassment, whoever they are, whatever
their background, identity, or abilities, and however much experience they have. Many people
who use and test SpeakEasy communicate with assistive technology. Respect the time and effort that
takes, and do not judge anyone by how quickly or in what way they communicate.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the people who rely on the software.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassing, insulting, or demeaning anyone, in public or in private.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, language, or imagery.
- Sharing someone's private information or messages without their permission.
- Any other behavior that would be out of place in a respectful professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other channels where you represent the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately. Open a private GitHub
security advisory on the repository, or contact the maintainers through the repository. Do not
report conduct problems in a public issue.

Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will respect the privacy and safety
of the person who reported it. Maintainers who do not follow the code of conduct may face
consequences decided by other members of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the enforcement guidelines in the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
correction, warning, temporary ban, and permanent ban, depending on the impact of the behavior.
